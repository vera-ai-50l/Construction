# Credits

Inside Job uses free 3D assets made by other people. Thank you!

## Characters
- **Security guard and supervisor** — [Blocky Characters 2.0 by Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters), CC0. The police and suited characters use recolored uniform textures, matte lighting, and their original idle, walk and sprint animations.

## Low-poly furniture and street dressing
- **Sofas** — [Sofa](https://poly.pizza/m/l84E3NzGSF) and [Sofa with cushions](https://poly.pizza/m/vuo7KBehok), Quaternius, CC0.
- **Chairs and stool** — [Cushioned chair](https://poly.pizza/m/IRLaR71Pyn), [Wood chair](https://poly.pizza/m/iMNqRzPwwe), and [Stool](https://poly.pizza/m/9QpPhjLcMV), Quaternius, CC0.
- **Houseplant and cone** — [Houseplant](https://poly.pizza/m/VtJh4Irl4w) and [Traffic Cone](https://poly.pizza/m/aDIrUbMbW3), Quaternius, CC0.
- Furniture and street assets were repacked and recolored for this build. Limestone tiles and entrance signage are generated by the game.
