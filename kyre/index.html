How it works

Four steps. You only see the good part.

  1. 1

    A 15-minute call

    We agree who you want to reach, what you're offering them, and exactly what counts as an interested lead. In writing.

  2. 2

    We set up sending

    New mailboxes in your company's name, warmed for 2 to 4 weeks before a single cold email goes out. Your everyday inbox is never touched.

  3. 3

    We run the outreach

    Lists built from live signals, like companies posting roles this week. Short, personal emails and follow-ups, with an opt-out in every one.

  4. 4

    You get the yeses

    Interested replies come to you with the full conversation, ready for your next step. That's when you pay, and only then.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is there really no setup fee or retainer?

None. Mailboxes, warmup, lists and copy are on us. You pay only for interested leads.

Whose name do the emails go out in?

Yours. Outreach goes out under your company's name, from mailboxes set up just for it, so replies feel like they came straight to you.

How soon do the first leads come in?

New mailboxes warm up for 2 to 4 weeks before outreach starts. Interested replies usually begin in the first weeks of sending.

Who decides whether a lead is interested?

We agree the definition in writing before we start, and you see every reply. If a lead doesn't meet it, you don't pay for it.

Which countries do you reach?

We check the rules for each country before building a list. Where cold email needs prior consent, such as Germany, we use LinkedIn instead.
