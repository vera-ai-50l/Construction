## What Kyre is

Kyre is a pay-per-lead outbound agency. We cold-email hiring companies (recruitment agencies, staffing firms, companies hiring directly) and pitch: we generate candidate and business leads for you, you pay only when a lead is positive or interested. No retainer, no upfront fee, pure performance pricing.

Once a company signs on as a client, we create new Google Workspace mailboxes under their own domain (not ours), warm those mailboxes up on our SalesHandy account over 2 to 4 weeks, then run cold outreach to candidates or targets as if sending from the client company itself. Replies that show real interest get handed to the client. We bill per positive lead delivered.

Kyre is a separate business from Vera AI (our construction-tech SaaS). Different market, different buyers, different brand.

## How it relates to the Two-Engine Hedge memo (docs/two-engine-hedge.md)

Kyre is the "lead gen (new brand)" engine in that memo: same niche (recruitment and staffing), same tools (Saleshandy workspaces, n8n + Apify signals), separate brand, domains and inboxes from Vera.
