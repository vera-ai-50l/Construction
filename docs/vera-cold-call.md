# Vera cold-call script: construction, MMR and Pune

20 Nov 2026 · Goes with `docs/vera-outreach.md` (buyer, signals, offer ladder) · Phone numbers come from the `vera-leads` export (`Phone` column) and Lead Finder.

**Goal of every call:** one site's WhatsApp messages for the free 2-hour site-record test, or a fixed time to talk again. Not a sale. Nobody buys software on a cold call. They agree to a free test.

Speak the way they speak. Most MDs switch between Hindi, Marathi and English. Each key line is below in English and Hinglish; use whichever comes naturally, and switch if they do.

---

## 1. Before you dial (60 seconds)

Have these open in the call sheet:

| Know | Example |
|---|---|
| Firm and city | Raj Constructions, Thane |
| The person (MD, owner, partner) | Rajesh Patil |
| The signal, in one line | New MahaRERA project "Sai Shraddha Heights" / completion date extended / past 75% complete / hiring 3 site engineers |
| Did we email them? | Yes, 4 days ago, no reply |

**Best times:** 10:30 am to 12:30 pm, and 4 to 6:30 pm. Early mornings they are on site; lunch and evenings they won't take it. No calls on Sundays or during festivals.

---

## 2. If the office or a receptionist picks up

The website number usually reaches the office, not the MD. Be short and specific; vague calls get blocked.

> **EN:** "Hi, this is {{Name}} from Vera. I'm calling for Rajesh sir, about site reporting on {{Project}}. Is he available?"
>
> **HI:** "Namaste, main {{Name}}, Vera se. Rajesh sir se baat karni thi, {{Project}} ki site reporting ke baare mein. Woh available hain?"

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "What is it about?" | "We help builders get the daily site report straight from the site's WhatsApp messages. Two minutes with sir is enough." |
| "Send an email" | "Sure. Which email does sir read himself? And what's a good time to call him back?" Note both. |
| "He's on site / in a meeting" | "When is the best time to catch him? I'll call then." Then call exactly then. |
| "Who handles site reports and material?" offered | Take the name and number. That person is often the project head and a good second contact. |

Never lie about why you're calling, never say "he asked me to call".

---

## 3. The opener (first 15 seconds)

Name, why them, why now, then ask for permission. The permission question lowers their guard.

> **EN:** "Hi Rajesh sir, {{Name}} from Vera. I saw {{Project}} came up on MahaRERA in Thane, congratulations. This is a cold call, so I'll be quick. Can I take 30 seconds to tell you why I called, and you decide if it's worth more?"
>
> **HI:** "Rajesh sir, namaste, main {{Name}}, Vera se. MahaRERA par {{Project}} dekha Thane mein, congratulations. Seedha bolta hoon, yeh cold call hai. 30 second doge? Phir aap decide karo aage baat karni hai ya nahi."

Opener line by signal (replace the MahaRERA line):

| Signal | Say |
|---|---|
| New project | "Saw {{Project}} on MahaRERA, congratulations on the launch." |
| Completion date extended | "Saw the completion date for {{Project}} moved on MahaRERA." (Neutral. Never say "your project is late".) |
| Past 75% complete | "Saw {{Project}} is past 75% complete on MahaRERA, so you're in the last stretch." |
| Hiring | "Saw you're hiring site engineers in Mumbai, so I guess the sites are growing." |
| We emailed earlier | Add: "I'd also sent you an email last week." |

## 4. The 30 seconds

> **EN:** "Most builders we speak to hear about site problems days late, and nobody can say exactly where the cement and steel went. Your site team already sends everything on WhatsApp: voice notes, photos, challans. Vera reads those messages and gives you a clean daily site record every morning, plus a material check: what came in, what was used, and what doesn't add up. No new app for the site team."
>
> **HI:** "Zyadatar builders ko site ki problem 2-3 din baad pata chalti hai, aur cement-steel kahan gaya, koi exactly nahi bata pata. Aapki site team already sab WhatsApp pe bhejti hai: voice note, photo, challan. Vera wohi messages padhke har subah ek clean site report deta hai, aur material check: kitna aaya, kitna laga, aur kahan gadbad hai. Site team ko koi naya app nahi seekhna."

Then stop talking and ask the first question.

---

## 5. Three questions (listen more than you talk)

1. **EN:** "How do site updates reach you today, and how long before you see them?"
   **HI:** "Abhi site ka update aap tak kaise aata hai, aur kitne time baad?"
2. **EN:** "How do you match the cement and steel issued with the work actually done? Who does it, and how often?"
   **HI:** "Cement aur steel jo issue hua, usko kaam ke saath kaise match karte ho? Kaun karta hai, kitni baar?"
3. **EN:** "Which site worries you most right now?"
   **HI:** "Abhi kaunsi site sabse zyada tension de rahi hai?"

Write down their exact words. Their answer to question 3 is the site you offer the test on.

If they mention a problem, ask one more: "What did that cost you last time?" / "Pichli baar usme kitna nuksaan hua?" Don't pitch on top of it. Let the number sit.

---

## 6. The close: the free 2-hour test

> **EN:** "Here's what I'd suggest. Forward us one day of WhatsApp messages from {{that site}}: voice notes, photos, challans, whatever comes. Within 2 hours we'll send back that day's site record: labour by contractor, work done, material received, and anything that doesn't add up. Free, no commitment. If it's useful, we talk. If not, no problem."
>
> **HI:** "Ek kaam karte hain. {{Us site}} ka ek din ka WhatsApp forward kar do, jo bhi aata hai: voice note, photo, challan. 2 ghante mein uss din ki site report bhej denge: kaunsa contractor, kitne labour, kya kaam hua, kya material aaya, aur kya match nahi ho raha. Free hai, koi commitment nahi. Kaam ka laga toh baat karenge, nahi toh koi baat nahi."

Lock it before you hang up:
- "Can I WhatsApp you on this number?" / "Isi number pe WhatsApp karoon?"
- Who will forward the messages (often the site engineer), and which day.
- A time to walk them through the result: "I'll send it by 2 pm. Can I call you at 4 to go through it?"

If they'd rather see it first: send the sample site record (labelled sample data) on WhatsApp right after the call, and ask for their site's messages in the same message.

**Bigger interest** (they mention material losses, disputes, or recovering excess from contractors): offer the one-package material check instead. "Pick one package. Share three months of POs, challans and issue slips under an NDA, and in a week we'll show, line by line in rupees, where material went beyond what the work needed. Also free." Never ask for project data without an NDA.

## 7. Objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "I'm busy" / "Busy hoon" | "Understood. Is tomorrow 11 am or 5 pm better? Two minutes." Book it and call exactly then. |
| "Send details on WhatsApp / email" | "Sure. Can I ask one question first so I send the right thing? How do your site reports reach you today?" Then send the sample, not a brochure. |
| "We have an ERP / SAP / an app" | "Good. Vera doesn't replace it. It takes what the site already sends on WhatsApp and turns it into entries your ERP and Tally can take. Does the site team actually fill the app every day?" |
| "My engineers handle it" | "They do the work. The question is how fast and in what shape it reaches you. The test shows that in 2 hours, free." |
| "Data is confidential" | "Fair. For the 2-hour test, pick any one day from any site, even an old one. For anything bigger we sign an NDA first, and we delete the data when we're done." |
| "How much does it cost?" | "The test is free. If you want it for your sites after that, we agree the price once you've seen it working. Material checks are a fixed fee plus a share of what you actually recover." Don't quote a monthly number on a cold call. |
| "Who else uses it?" | Tell the truth: "We're early. We're running it with our first few builders now, which is why the test is free." Never name a client or a relationship without permission. |
| "Not interested" | "No problem, thanks for your time. Can I ask, is site reporting just not a problem for you, or is it the timing?" If still no: thank them, end the call, mark it, never call again. |
| "Don't call me again" | "Sorry for the trouble, I'll take your number off our list." Do it the same day (`node vera-leads.mjs suppress`). |

---

## 8. No answer

- Don't leave voicemail. Call at most 3 times, on different days and at different times.
- After the second missed call, send one WhatsApp:

> "Hello Rajesh ji, {{Name}} from Vera. Tried calling about {{Project}}. We turn a site's WhatsApp messages into a daily site record and material check. Happy to run a free 2-hour test on one site. Is there a good time to talk? 🙏"

- No reply after the third try: back to email only.

## 9. After every call (1 minute)

Log it in the call sheet the same minute:

| Column | Example |
|---|---|
| Date, caller | 20 Nov, Rishiraj |
| Firm, person, number | Raj Constructions, Rajesh Patil, office line |
| Reached | MD / gatekeeper / no answer |
| Outcome | Test agreed / callback booked / send sample / not now (check back date) / no / don't call |
| Their words | "Reports come at night, half the time from memory" |
| Next step and date | Send test result by 2 pm 21 Nov, call 4 pm |

Every Friday (with the email numbers in `docs/vera-outreach.md`): dials, MDs reached, tests agreed, tests run. Change one thing in the script a week, never three.

---

## 10. Rules

- **Calls go with email, not instead of it.** Call firms we've emailed first, and firms with a fresh signal. A call that names their project lands; a random one doesn't.
- **Keep volume low and every call personal.** TRAI's rules on promotional calls allow numbers to be blacklisted or disconnected after recipient complaints. Call office and business numbers first, never call anyone who asked us to stop, and don't use a calling robot or a script that reads like one. Before calling at volume (say 50+ a day), check the current TRAI rules and whether we need to register as a telemarketer.
- **Never name a client or a warm relationship** on a call, in a message or in marketing.
- **Facts only.** Say what MahaRERA shows, never "your project is delayed" or a guess about their problems.
- **No real project data without an NDA** (beyond the one-day test they choose to forward). Sample files are fictional and labelled as samples.
- **Reply to every follow-up the same day.** The test result goes back within 2 hours, as promised.
