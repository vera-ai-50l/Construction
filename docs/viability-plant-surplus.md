# Desks 04 and 05: are they worth building?

Short answer: **yes, as add-ons to the Site Record Desk. Not as standalone products.** Lead with findings that have a hard rupee figure, not with "preventive maintenance".

## 04 Plant & Formwork Desk

**Where the money goes** (mid-size contractor, ₹100–300 Cr turnover, typically ₹5–30 Cr of owned plant and formwork):

| Leak | Typical size | Provable? |
|---|---|---|
| Hired equipment idle on rent | ₹2–4k/day per machine; idle stretches of a week are common | **Hard ₹**: the hire invoice and the hour-meter |
| Owned machine idle at one site while another site hires | One month of hire for each case caught | **Hard ₹** |
| Formwork and scaffolding lost between sites and subcontractors | 5–10% of the stock per project | **Hard ₹**, if charged back before the contractor leaves |
| Diesel above the litres-per-hour norm | 10–25% on unmonitored DGs and machines | Strong signal; needs follow-up |
| Breakdowns after missed services | One major repair plus idle crews | Avoided cost; hard to prove |
| Lapsed load-test, insurance or fitness certificates | Work stoppage, penalties | Avoided cost |

**Why it fits Vera:**
- Operators and supervisors already send hour-meter, diesel and transfer photos on WhatsApp, so there is nothing new to learn.
- It uses the same site master and the same 8 AM brief as desk 02.

**Risks:**
- Preventive-maintenance software is a crowded market, and avoided-breakdown savings are hard to show to an MD.
- It needs an asset register set up once. Most contractors have it scattered across Excel files.
- It depends on a daily hour-meter photo. It must be part of the existing site group habit, or it stops after a month.

**Recommendation:**
- Offer it as an add-on to the Site Record Desk.
- First features to build: off-hire alerts, the owned-idle vs hiring-elsewhere check, and the formwork tally. These pay for the month on their own.
- Add service due and certificates on top, since they reuse the same hour data.

## 05 Surplus Stock Exchange

**Where the money goes:**
- Stock is bought new at one site while another site holds the same item.
- Cement ages past 90 days in a damp store.
- Admixtures and adhesives expire.
- End-of-project leftovers are sold as scrap at 30–50% of cost.

**Why it fits Vera:** it is built entirely from data that desks 01 and 02 already read: stores registers, BOQ progress, purchase requests and POs. The before-PO check is a single question the purchase team can act on the same day.

**Risks:**
- It only pays with 3 or more live sites within a sensible transport radius.
- Book stock has to be corrected by periodic physical counts.

**Recommendation:**
- Offer it to any client on the Site Record Desk with three or more sites.
- The example on the page is ₹83,600 of PO avoided for ₹3,400 of transport, which is typical of what a single match saves.
