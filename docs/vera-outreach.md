# Vera outreach strategy: construction, MMR and Pune

13 Nov 2026 · Vera engine of the Two-Engine Hedge (`docs/two-engine-hedge.md`) · Tool: `vera-leads/`

## The answer

Sell to **mid-size construction contractors and developers in MMR and Pune** through two lanes at once:

1. **Warm lane (first claim on time):** every Lodha conversation ends with a request for 2–3 introductions to mid-size contractors on their sites. Warm intros close faster than anything cold.
2. **Cold signal lane:** each Monday, `vera-leads` finds firms with a public reason to care **this month**: a new MahaRERA project, a completion date extended, a deadline close, or a hiring push for site staff. The first email names that event.

Lead with a free, two-hour proof (their own site record from one day of WhatsApp messages), then a free one-package material check, then a paid pilot.

At the memo's planning rates the cold lane adds **about one paying client a month** from early 2027. Signal firms always go first; Saleshandy Lead Finder lists fill the rest of the 150 a week the three cold inboxes can carry.

**Gate (Vera's own):** by **15 Jan 2027**, 600 firms contacted with at least **6 positive replies and 3 site-record tests run**. Below that, change the opener or the offer once before adding volume.

## 1. Who we sell to

| | In | Out |
|---|---|---|
| Firm | Contractors and developers, roughly ₹50–500 Cr revenue, 2+ live sites | Listed and large developers, EPC majors (long procurement, SAP), single-plot one-off builders |
| Place | MMR (Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai, Palghar, Raigad) and Pune | Rest of Maharashtra for now (scored lower, not dropped) |
| Buyer | MD, owner, founder or partner. Often also the project head. | HR, admin, junior engineers (they don't buy, and email to them burns contacts) |
| Never cold | Lodha and Macrotech: warm relationship, intros only | Anyone on the do-not-contact list |

The pain we sell to, in their words: site reports arrive late and in different formats, the MD hears about problems days after they happen, and nobody can say where the cement and steel went. Vera reads what the site team already sends on WhatsApp (voice notes in Hindi or Marathi, Hinglish texts, challan photos) and returns a daily site record by morning, plus a material check: what came in, what was issued, and what the work actually needed.

## 2. Signals and what each one means

| Signal (vera-leads finds it) | Why now | First line (auto-written) | Best freebie |
|---|---|---|---|
| New MahaRERA registration | Before the first pour, process is still being set | "Saw *Sai Shraddha Heights* come up on MahaRERA in Thane. Congratulations on the launch." | 2-hour site-record test |
| Completion date extended | The project is late by definition; the MD wants control | "Saw the MahaRERA completion date for *Green Acres II* has moved to March 2027." | Material check on one package |
| Completion due within 6 months, or past | Last stretch, where delay and overrun cost most | "*Raj Residency* is due for completion on MahaRERA by February 2027." | Material check on one package |
| Hiring site, planning, billing, QS or stores staff | More sites than the team can report on | "Saw *Raj Constructions* is hiring for 3 site roles in Mumbai, including a site engineer." | 2-hour site-record test |

Tone for MahaRERA signals: public facts, neutral, never "your project is late". Congratulate launches; state dates plainly.

The memo's planning assumption is 650 MahaRERA events a month in Maharashtra, of which about a quarter fit (region and size). Hiring adds firms MahaRERA misses: contractors don't register projects, developers do.

| Step | What they get | Cost to them | Our effort |

Rules: never ask for real project data without an NDA. Sample files use fictional firms and are labelled as samples. Agree what we keep and when we delete it.

## 4. The 14-day sequence

Two channels done well: email and LinkedIn. Calls and WhatsApp **only after they engage** (TRAI rules make cold calls from personal numbers risky). A reply on any channel stops every other step.

| Day | Channel | Step |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | LinkedIn | Connect with the MD/owner, no note |
| 2 | Email 1 | Their signal + what Vera does, under 80 words, no link |
| 4 | LinkedIn | If accepted: one-line DM on the same signal |
| 6 | Email 2 | Offer the 2-hour site-record test |
| 8 | Call | Only if they replied, accepted, or came via intro |
| 10 | WhatsApp | Sample site record, only after they engaged |
| 14 | Email 3 | Close the loop, ask for a referral |

### Email 1 (day 2)

Subject options (A/B test, lower case, 2–4 words): `site records at {{Company}}` · `{{Project}}` · `quick one, {{First Name}}`

> Hi {{First Name}},
>
> {{Signal Line}} {{Angle}}
>
> Vera turns your site team's WhatsApp messages and challan photos into a daily site record by next morning, with material checked against work done.
>
> Worth a 15-minute look?
>
> {{Sender}}, Vera AI
>
> Not relevant? Reply "no" and I won't write again.

With the merge fields filled in, this comes to 65–80 words. Example, for a completion-date extension:

> Hi Sunil,

>
> Vera turns your site team's WhatsApp messages and challan photos into a daily site record by next morning, with material checked against work done.

> Worth a 15-minute look?

> Hi {{First Name}},
>

>

### LinkedIn DM (day 4, if accepted)

> Thanks for connecting, {{First Name}}. Saw {{Project}} on MahaRERA. We help builders in Mumbai and Pune get the daily site record and material check straight from site WhatsApp, without extra staff. Happy to run a free 2-hour test on one site if useful.

For a hiring signal, replace the second sentence with: "Saw you're hiring for site roles."

### WhatsApp (day 10, only after they engaged)

> Hello {{First Name}} ji, {{Sender}} from Vera here, following up on our email. Sharing a sample site record (sample data) so you can see the format. If you forward one day's messages from any one site, we'll send back yours within 2 hours. Thank you 🙏

### Call (day 8, only if engaged)

Open in one line: who you are and the signal. Then three questions, and listen:

1. How do site updates reach you today, and how long before you see them?
2. How do you reconcile cement and steel issued against work done? Who does it, and how often?
3. Which site worries you most right now?

Close: offer the 2-hour test on that site, and fix a time to send it back.

### Replies: what to send back (same day, always)

| They say | Reply |
|---|---|
| Interested / call me | Book within 24 hours. Send two time slots and a Google Meet link. |
| Send details | Three lines + the sample site record + "happy to run yours in 2 hours". |
| We use ERP / SAP / an app | "Vera doesn't replace it. It takes what the site already sends on WhatsApp and puts it into records your ERP and Tally can take." |
| Not now | "When should I check back?" Set a task for that date. |
| Wrong person | Ask who handles site reporting and material, and thank them. |
| Unsubscribe / no | Thank them, then `vera-leads suppress` the same day. |

## 5. Sending setup

The three inboxes on **getveraai.com** (Shivam, Rishiraj, Aditya) are the main brand domain, the one on proposals and the Lodha thread. Per the memo, nothing high-volume goes out from it.

| Inboxes | Use | Daily cap per inbox |
|---|---|---|
| 3 on getveraai.com (already running) | Replies, warm intros, follow-ups with firms that replied. No cold lists. | 15 |
| 3 new cold inboxes on 1 secondary domain (similar name, redirecting to getveraai.com) | Cold sequences: `saleshandy.csv` and `saleshandy-list.csv` | Ramp to 30 |

- Buy 1 secondary domain and 3 Google Workspace inboxes this week (about ₹1,100 a month plus about ₹800 a year). SPF, DKIM and DMARC on each, same steps as getveraai.com.
- Ramp per cold inbox: weeks 1–3 warm-up only, then 5–10 a day, 15, 20, and 30 by about week 6. 30 is a ceiling, not a start.
- At 30 a day: 3 × 30 × 5 days = **450 sends a week**. A 3-email sequence uses 3 sends per person, so that is **150 new people a week** (about 650 a month). `vera-leads export` is capped at 150 for this reason (`perExport` in `config.mjs`).
- Two sources fill those 150: signal firms first (`saleshandy.csv`), then Saleshandy Lead Finder lists (`saleshandy-list.csv`). Keep them as two separate sequences so you can see which one gets replies.
- Saleshandy: one workspace for Vera, separate from Kyre. Plain text, open tracking off, no links in email 1, sender is a real founder.
- Hard limits: bounce under 2% (the tool exports only verified emails), spam complaints under 0.1%. Any campaign over 2% bounce is paused. If the cold domain gets burned, getveraai.com is untouched: buy a new one and carry on.
- To grow later, add a second secondary domain with 2–3 more inboxes. Don't push existing inboxes past 30.

### Lead Finder lists (no signal)

Filter Lead Finder to construction or real estate, Mumbai, Thane, Navi Mumbai and Pune, 11–500 employees, titles MD, Director, Founder, Owner, Partner. Export as CSV, then:

```bash
node vera-leads.mjs contacts leadfinder-list.csv --list
node vera-leads.mjs verify
node vera-leads.mjs export
```

List firms go through the same checks as signal firms: verified email only, Lodha and the large developers removed, opt-outs, and the 90-day rule. If a list firm also has a MahaRERA or hiring signal, it gets the signal email instead. Their first line is neutral, since there is no event to name:

> Came across Desai Buildtech while looking at construction firms in Thane. On most sites, the office hears about problems days after they happen.

Expect fewer replies than the signal sequence. If the list sequence is under 1% replies after 300 people, stop buying lists and put the capacity back into signals and LinkedIn.

## 8. Rules that protect the business

- **Vera's live conversations come first.** A Vera reply never waits over 24 hours.
- **Kill trigger:** a getveraai.com email lands in spam, or the domain shows on a blacklist. Stop all cold sending until fixed.
- **Lodha:** never use its name, logo or projects in outreach or marketing. Introductions only.
- **Opt-outs:** in every email, honoured the same day, one shared list with Kyre (`vera-leads/data/suppression.txt`).
- **India DPDP Rules, from 13 May 2027:** named work emails become personal data with consent at the centre. `vera-leads` already records where every contact and signal came from. Plan to move toward intros, events (CREDAI-MCHI, Pune builders' meets) and inbound by spring 2027.
- **Calls and WhatsApp** only after someone has engaged, never cold.
- **Scraping:** gentle on MahaRERA (one page every 3 seconds), public data only, and scraped data never goes into git.
